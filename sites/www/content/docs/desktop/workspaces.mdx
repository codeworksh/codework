## Workspaces

Each workspace is a tab in the title bar. Press **+** to add one, up to nine, and ⌘1 to ⌘9 to switch between them. Rename, reorder, and delete them in [Settings → Workspaces](/docs/desktop/settings).

Closing a tab with its **×** doesn't ask first.

## Panes

Every open session is a pane. ⌘-click a session in the sidebar to open it beside the others: one pane takes the main column, and the rest stack beside it.

| Button       | What it does                                           |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Pin**      | Keeps the pane in place, so nothing else opens over it |
| **Maximize** | Shows this pane alone; press again to tile             |
| **Move**     | Sends the pane to another workspace, or a new one      |
| **Close**    | Closes the pane                                        |

Drag a pane by its header to reorder it. Drag the line between the columns to resize them, or focus it and press ← or →. When the stack runs out of room, older panes fold down to their headers, and the rest wait in a **+N more** menu.

Your workspaces, panes, and column widths come back when the app restarts.
