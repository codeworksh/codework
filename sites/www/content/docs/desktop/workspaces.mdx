## Workspaces

Each workspace is a tab in the title bar. Press **+** to add one, up to nine, and ⌘1 to ⌘9 to switch between them. Rename, reorder, and delete them in [Settings → Workspaces](/docs/desktop/settings).

Closing a tab with its **×** doesn't ask first.

## Frames

Every open session is a frame. ⌘-click a session in the sidebar to open it beside the others: one frame takes the main column, and the rest stack beside it.

| Button       | What it does                                            |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pin**      | Keeps the frame in place, so nothing else opens over it |
| **Maximize** | Shows this frame alone; press again to tile             |
| **Move**     | Sends the frame to another workspace, or a new one      |
| **Close**    | Closes the frame                                        |

Drag a frame by its header to reorder it. Drag the line between the columns to resize them, or focus it and press ← or →. When the stack runs out of room, older frames fold down to their headers, and the rest wait in a **+N more** menu.

Your workspaces, frames, and column widths come back when the app restarts.
